Hi all! Here you can find some information about my Spark AR filters.
By clicking on each filter you will be redirected to a page where you will be asked if you want to try the filter by opening it on Instagram.
After that, Instagram will open the camera of your smartphone and you can finally use it.
Have fun and share my filters with your friends!


If you post a photo or a video using them, don't forget to tag @lucke94 (Follow me!) so that I can repost you.

NB: Cocktail Predictor filter can only be used in test mode as it is not accepted by the Spark AR Policies, however I made it available for you.
If you like it and you are of legal age, share it and tag me*!

*I do not take any responsibility for its incorrect use.
